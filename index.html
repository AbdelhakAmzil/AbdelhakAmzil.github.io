Frequently asked questions

Still unsure? Ask me directly.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on the scope. After a short call or message, I send a fixed price for the whole project, split into milestones. Small fixes and ongoing improvements can be billed by the hour.

How long does it take?

The quote includes a schedule with a date for each milestone. You see progress at every step, not only at the end.

Can we work through Upwork or Fiverr?

Yes. You can hire me on Upwork or Fiverr and use their payment protection, or work with me directly with milestone payments.

Which technologies do you use?

Java and Spring Boot for the back-end, Angular for the web front-end, mobile apps for iOS and Android, PostgreSQL, Docker, Odoo for ERP, and Python with LLMs for AI features. I pick stable, well-supported tools so your app lasts.

What happens after the launch?

You get the code, the documentation and the access. I stay available for fixes and new features, by the hour or with a monthly support plan.

Do you work with clients outside Morocco?

Yes, remotely. I am based in Casablanca (GMT) and work with clients in English, French or Arabic.
