Case study · E-commerce platform · 2026

Mall Privé: private sales end to end, from the order to the delivered parcel.

A Moroccan platform that brings together a customer website, a portal for brands and a back-office for the team, with payment, preparation at the hub and delivery by the Chrono Diali carrier.

Role
Freelance full-stack engineer, from design to delivery
Client
Moroccan private-sales startup
Scope
Back-end, 3 front-ends, infrastructure
Status
In acceptance testing
Large screenshot: customer site home page (sample data)

The need

Sell partner brands' collections online at reduced prices, for a few days at a time. The platform had to handle Moroccan payments (card and cash on delivery), multi-brand orders, receiving parcels at the hub, delivery and tracking, with personal data protected under Moroccan law 09-08.

What I built

  • A Spring Boot microservices architecture connected by Kafka
  • Three Angular apps: customer site, vendor portal, back-office
  • Secure sign-in with Keycloak and two-factor authentication for the team
  • Integration of the Chrono Diali carrier (shipping, tracking, pickups)
  • CSV exports and PDF documents (receiving notes)

Technical choices

Recent, stable tools, chosen to last.

Back-end

Java 25, Spring Boot 4, PostgreSQL 18, Flyway. Kafka events published through an outbox: no message is lost.

Front-end

Angular 22 with signals, three apps tested with Vitest; customer site in French and Arabic.

Security

Keycloak (3 realms), a BFF with no token in the browser, two-factor sign-in for the team, role-based access.

Quality

Integration tests with Testcontainers, one branch per task, documentation kept up to date.
